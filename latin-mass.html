Traditional Worship

Latin Mass at Blessed Sacrament

Join us for reverent celebration of the Traditional Latin Mass on Sundays, weekdays, and first Friday and first Saturday devotions.

Sunday Latin Mass

7:30 a.m. and 10:45 a.m. each Sunday.

Weekday Latin Mass

Tuesday and Thursday at 8:30 a.m. in the Rectory Chapel.

First Friday

Latin Low Mass at 6:30 p.m.

First Saturday

Latin Mass at 8:30 a.m., followed by altar server training.
