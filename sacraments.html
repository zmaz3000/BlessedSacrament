Sacraments

Encounter Christ Through the Sacraments

This page gives parishioners one place for sacramental preparation details, scheduling notes, and parish contact information.

Baptism

Celebrated by appointment. Please contact the pastor to begin preparation and scheduling.

Parents and godparents should complete the baptism registration form and preparation requirements in advance.

Baptism Registration

Eucharist

For First Communion and Confirmation preparation, please contact the Director of Religious Education.

Reconciliation

Confessions are heard Saturday 3:30-3:55 p.m., and Sunday 7:00-7:25, 8:30-8:55, and 10:15-10:40 a.m.

Confirmation

Preparation is available for youth and those completing sacramental formation in the parish.

Marriage

Arrangements should be made with the pastor at least six months in advance. Marriages are for active parishioners only.

Anointing of the Sick

Please contact the parish office for serious illness, pastoral care, and urgent sacramental needs.
