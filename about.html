About Blessed Sacrament

Welcome to Our Parish Family

Blessed Sacrament Catholic Church gathers parishioners and visitors for reverent worship, faithful Catholic formation, and life in Christ centered on the Eucharist.

Our Mission

We seek to know, love, and serve Christ through the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass, devotion to the sacraments, and works of mercy in parish life and beyond.

Visitors are always welcome at Blessed Sacrament. If you would like to join the parish, parish census forms are available at the Religious Goods Counter in the vestibule of the front entrance.
